Your secrets. One passphrase.
Zero lockouts.

OnlyLogins is a free and open source password manager that keeps your usernames, passwords and other login credentials secure. Your data is encrypted by your own passphrase and cannot be accessed without it. No 2FA, passkeys, or any other third-party account is required.

What's inside

Open source

The AGPL source code is free to read, audit, and self-host. Run your own instance locally and keep your data on hardware you control.

One passphrase

A single strong 24+ character passphrase replaces 2FA codes, hardware passkeys, and email verifications. As long as you remember your passphrase, your data stays secure and accessible.

Organize

Keep your usernames, passwords, and other login credentials organized into categorized folders. Never lose track of anything again.

What you can store

Account Logins

Store website login information including usernames and passwords. Keep track of website links so you never get lost.

Private Notes

Write down private information in a digital secure note.

Backup codes

Stash backup codes, license keys, Wi-Fi passwords, and other secret items in an organized document.

Self Host

Operate an OnlyLogins instance on your own hardware and maintain total soverignty over your data.
