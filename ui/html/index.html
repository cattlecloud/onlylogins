OnlyLogins is a free and open source password manager for usernames, passwords, and the other credentials you need. Everything is encrypted with your own passphrase. No 2FA, no passkeys, and no third-party account required.

What you can store

Account logins
Website URLs, usernames, and passwords, kept together so you never lose track of which credential belongs where.
Private notes
Free-form notes for anything that does not fit a login: recovery instructions, account details, or reminders.
Backup codes
Recovery codes, license keys, Wi-Fi passwords, and other secrets that need a safe place to live.

Why OnlyLogins

Open source

The AGPL source code is free to read, audit, and self-host. Run your own instance and keep your data on hardware you control.

One passphrase

A single strong passphrase replaces 2FA codes, hardware passkeys, and email verifications. Remember it, and your data stays both secure and accessible.

Organize better
