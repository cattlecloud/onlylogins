Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13th, 2026

CattleCloud LLC ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you access our website and services (the "Service"). OnlyLogins is a password manager: the secret values you store are encrypted with a key derived from your passphrase, and we cannot read them. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand our views and practices regarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use the Service:

  • Account information: email address, username, and your notification and marketing preferences.
  • Authentication data: a per-user salt and a verifier used to check your passphrase during login. We do not store your passphrase or the encryption key derived from it.
  • Encrypted vault data: the usernames, passwords, links, key/value pairs, and notes you store, in encrypted form. Folder and document names are stored in plain text so the Service can organize them.
  • Billing information: if you subscribe to the Patreon plan, payments are handled by Stripe. We receive limited information such as your plan status, subscription identifiers, and customer identifiers. We do not receive or store full payment card details.
  • Usage and security data: IP address, browser user agent, login attempts and timestamps, and access metadata for one-time share links.
  • Communications: messages you send to support and emails we send to you about the Service.
  • Cookies: session and encryption cookies required to keep you logged in and to use your encryption key during your session. We do not use advertising cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Service.
  • Authenticate your account and decrypt your vault during your session.
  • Enforce usage limits, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot problems.
  • Process Patreon subscriptions and manage billing.
  • Send service and security notifications, and send marketing communications only if you have opted in.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: trusted third parties that help us operate the Service, such as Stripe for payment processing, hosting and infrastructure providers, and email delivery providers. These providers are subject to confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Legal compliance: to comply with applicable laws, legal processes, or enforceable governmental requests.
  • Business transfers: if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

We cannot share the encrypted secrets in your vault because we cannot decrypt them.

4. User Control and Data Deletion

You may export a complete copy of your data at any time using the export feature. The export file is not encrypted and contains every secret in your account, so store it securely.

You may request deletion of your account at any time by contacting us. When your account is deleted, we will delete your account and associated data, subject to reasonable backup retention periods and legal obligations. Deleting your account is irreversible and may result in loss of access to the Service and its contents.

5. Security of Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data. Secret values are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase. Your passphrase and the derived key are never stored by us. While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure. You share your information with us at your own risk.

There is no passphrase recovery mechanism. If you forget your passphrase, no one, including OnlyLogins support, can restore access to your encrypted data. Folder and document names are stored in plain text, so do not use them to store sensitive information.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy or as required by law. If you delete your account or request data deletion, we will delete your data from our systems, subject to reasonable backup retention periods. Billing and subscription records may be retained as required by law or for accounting purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

Subject to your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • The right to access your data.
  • The right to correct inaccuracies.
  • The right to request deletion.
  • The right to data portability.

To exercise these rights, contact us for support. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The "Effective Date" at the top of this page indicates when the Policy was last revised. We may provide notice of material changes by email or through the Service.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact seth@cattlecloud.net for support. To report a security issue, please contact us privately before disclosing the details publicly.
